\opening{Dear Admissions Committee,}

I am applying for the Bachelor's in Computer Science at the Università di Camerino for the September 2027 intake.

I am a senior software engineer with seven years of professional experience across ERP, fintech, and education-technology platforms. Currently I am the sole engineer building a manufacturer customer portal at Barriertek, a fire-retardant lumber producer in Toronto: NestJS API, React + TanStack Query frontend, bi-directional NetSuite integration via SuiteScript RESTlets, PostgreSQL on Linux. The portal is live with internal users; full external rollout is in October 2026. I also maintain an open-source TypeScript client for NetSuite RESTlets at \href{https://github.com/Ahmad-b1995/netsuite-restlet-typescript}{github.com/Ahmad-b1995/netsuite-restlet-typescript}.

I trained in the Humanities track in Iran and earned my Pre-University Certificate in 2014. I started programming professionally instead of pursuing the Konkur examination, and the practical path has shaped my career since. I have studied algorithms and computer-science theory independently while working, but I now want the structured grounding that only a formal degree can provide.

Camerino specifically interests me for the size and focus of the programme, the faculty-to-student ratio, and the cost-of-living profile that allows a non-EU student moving with a partner to settle without financial strain. I have read the programme syllabus and the work of the School of Science and Technology; the curriculum's grounding in mathematics, algorithms, and software engineering is exactly the framework I need.

During the programme I will continue contract software work in my specialty (ERP integration), using the twenty hours per week allowed under the student visa. After graduation I plan to remain in the EU and build a small consulting practice for traditional industrial businesses going through digital transformation.

I would be grateful for the opportunity to study at Camerino.

\closing{Sincerely,}
