# Pre-application inquiry — German universities / Studienkollegs

For Germany, the process is two-step for someone without a Konkur entrance
result: enrol in a Studienkolleg for one preparatory year (T-Kurs for
engineering/CS), pass the Feststellungsprüfung, then apply to the actual
Bachelor's.

You can pre-inquire with BOTH the Studienkolleg AND your target university.

## Notes specific to Germany

- **Most German universities use uni-assist** as a clearing house for
  international applications. You upload documents once; uni-assist forwards
  to the universities you select. Fees are ~€75 first university, ~€30 each
  additional. The pre-inquiry is informational and does NOT replace the
  uni-assist submission.

- **DAAD scholarships** open well before the semester you apply for. The
  STIBET I (semester-cost subsidy) and the DAAD Master Programmes are the
  main funding sources. For Bachelor's: DAAD funding is rarer; primary path
  is the Deutschlandstipendium (merit, €300/month).

- **English-taught Bachelor's in Computer Science** are limited in Germany.
  The vast majority of Informatik bachelor's are in German. If you want
  English at undergrad level, the Studienkolleg path is unavoidable —
  Studienkolleg gives you the German.

- **Free tuition**: confirmed for non-EU students in all states except
  Baden-Württemberg (€1,500/semester). Berlin, Hamburg, Saxony, Bavaria,
  NRW (Aachen, Köln, Bonn) are all free.

- **Studienkolleg admission test**: typically a written exam in German
  (basic), math, and a short interview. Some Studienkollegs have English
  intake; verify with each.
